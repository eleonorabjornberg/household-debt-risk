- **The countries cannot be ranked yet.** Only the US has a headline share, and every gap elsewhere (student loans in all four countries; private insurers' federally backed cover in Canada; UK schemes) can only raise the other countries' shares. The evidence so far is qualitative: in Denmark "the credit risk is initially borne by the mortgage bank" (Danmarks Nationalbank, 2013), on mortgage-bank loans that make up 80.4% of Danish bank and mortgage-bank loans to households; in KfW's home-ownership program 124/134 the on-lending bank takes the liability ("die Haftung", KfW program sheet, 2023); Canada's public exposure runs through mortgage insurance (CMHC's 19.8% is a share of residential mortgages, which include multi-unit rental, not of household credit).
