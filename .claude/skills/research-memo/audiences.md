# Audiences

Eleonora's decision (2026-11-03, PLAN.md "Decisions taken"): memos target financial institutions:
banks, asset managers, insurers, MBS and CRT investors. Pick one profile per memo, or write a new one here
first, with the same fields.

| Key | Reader | What they decide | What they want from us | Lead with | Vocabulary they expect | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `mbs_desk` | Agency MBS trader or PM | Spread, coupon and prepayment positioning; supply forecasts | Who holds mortgages and how that is shifting: GSE and Ginnie pools vs banks, Fed runoff, private-label's share | Holder mix and Fed MBS runoff | agency pools, Ginnie, runoff, private-label, net supply | Policy advocacy; credit-score detail they already have |
| `rates_strategist` | Rates or macro strategist at an asset manager | Duration and curve views; what the Fed balance sheet does next | Fed MBS holdings and runoff path, household leverage vs income, how fast public holdings respond to shocks | Fed MBS share and runoff; debt to income | QT, balance-sheet runoff, leverage, household sector | Loan-level credit talk |
| `bank_treasury` | Bank treasury / credit-risk team | Balance-sheet mix, mortgage retention vs sale, consumer-credit appetite | Banks' share of household debt over time, collateral coverage, where unsecured credit is growing | Banks' share and collateral coverage | retention, originate-to-distribute, coverage, unsecured, risk transfer | Trading jargon |
| `insurer_alm` | Insurer ALM / fixed-income team | Allocation to agency vs private-label vs consumer ABS | Where private holders still carry household credit risk and how that changed after 2008 | Private-label collapse and private share | private-label, ABS, credit risk transfer, spread | Short-term trading views |
| `crt_investor` | CRT (credit risk transfer) investor | Appetite for GSE CRT deals | How much mortgage credit risk sits with the GSEs, and what conservatorship release would change | GSE/agency share; what to watch | CRT, conservatorship, guarantee, first loss | Consumer-credit detail |
| `policy_shop` | Policy researcher (outside the decided target; use only if Eleonora asks) | Public-finance and housing-policy positions | Public exposure and its history | Public share and the 2008 ratchet | backstop, contingent liability | Trading views |
