**Why one metric does not fit all five.** The US public share is mostly guarantees on loans held by others; Canada's is insurance on loans held privately; Germany's large public-law lenders are owned by the public sector but, after 2005, are not backed by a state guarantee (Bundesbank, `Q7_EVIDENCE.md`). The holder measure (a) and the risk-bearer measure (b) therefore rank the countries differently, which is why both are shown.
