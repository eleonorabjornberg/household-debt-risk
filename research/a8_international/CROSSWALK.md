# A8 definition crosswalk

> **Claude draft, not the author's text.** What "public" means in each system for the two measures Eleonora chose (2026-11-04): (b) a public body takes the credit loss (headline); (a) a publicly owned lender holds the loan. Cells marked **unverified** rest on general knowledge and have no source in `sources.md` yet.

| | (b) Who takes the credit loss on a typical loan | (a) Publicly owned holders | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **US** | The GSEs (guarantee on their MBS) and FHA/VA (insurance) for agency loans; the federal government on federal student loans; otherwise the private holder. CRT and mortgage insurance pass part of the GSEs' loss to private investors (A2). | Federal government, GSE and FHLB retained portfolios, state and local governments; guaranteed MBS are mostly held privately. | Sourced: Z.1, FHFA, Freddie 10-K (A1, A2). Holder share not separable after FAS 166/167. |
| **Canada** | The lender, unless the loan is insured: CMHC (Crown corporation) or a private insurer whose cover carries a partial federal backstop. | Not sourced (holders of NHA MBS and Canada Mortgage Bonds). | CMHC share of residential mortgages sourced; private-insurer backstop **unverified**. |
| **Denmark** | The mortgage bank on realkredit loans: "the credit risk is initially borne by the mortgage bank" (Danmarks Nationalbank, 2013); the state on SU student loans. | The state (SU student loans); no public mortgage lender found. | Mortgage banks sourced; SU loans **not sourced**. |
| **UK** | The lender on bank and building-society loans; the government on student loans, as junior lender on Help to Buy equity loans, and partly under the Mortgage Guarantee Scheme. | The government (student loans, Help to Buy equity loans). | Bank of England totals (excluding student loans) sourced; government schemes **unverified** and not quantified. |
| **Germany** | The lending bank, including on KfW programme 124/134 loans (the on-lending bank takes the liability, KfW 2023); the state on BAföG loans. Lending by publicly owned savings banks, Landesbanken and promotional banks is a third category, "publicly owned, not guaranteed" (Eleonora, Q7, 2026-11-04; evidence in `Q7_EVIDENCE.md`). | Savings banks, Landesbanken and promotional banks (Bundesbank table I.7); public building-savings banks not separable. | Holder share sourced; end of state guarantees for savings banks (2005) **unverified**; BAföG **not sourced**. |

**Why one metric does not fit all five.** The US public share is mostly guarantees on loans held by others; Canada's is insurance on loans held privately; Germany's large public-law lenders are owned by the public sector but, after 2005, are not (on general knowledge, unverified) backed by a state guarantee. The holder measure (a) and the risk-bearer measure (b) therefore rank the countries differently, which is why both are shown.
