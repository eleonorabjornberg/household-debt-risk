# E1 question-to-SQL benchmark: results (draft)

Run `run-2026-11-05b`: 80 scored questions (60 answerable, 20 unanswerable) × Haiku, Sonnet and Opus 5.5 × 3 repetitions = 720 tool-use loops through the household-credit MCP tools (snapshot backend, Z.1 vintage 2026-09-11). It completed with no stop and 0 API errors. The 20 held-out questions were not run.

**Read with the deviations.** The first 79 loops ran before D4 (the recalibrated stops), under D2 and D3; the run was resumed from loop 80 under D4 and D5. The scorer fix D1 applied to every loop. The pilot (10 easy questions) was seen before the design was fixed. Claude wrote the questions (CHOICE-E1-3 (a)), so their style may favour Claude models.

## What the misses were (checked by Claude, answer by answer; no scorer artifacts)

- Opus declined three questions that compare NY Fed and Z.1 figures (q128, q173, q175) six times, arguing the two sources are not comparable. The frozen rule scores these as wrong.
- Several loops hit the 4-turn limit while still fetching data (q122 for all three models; q184 for Opus and Sonnet).
- Four answers of 36.2 on q156 (gold 36.216, tolerance $0.01 trillion) miss by rounding to one decimal, under the tolerance in the approved set.
